##### Explanation:

The grayscale image can be reconstructed as a sum of components obtained from its SVD. The singular values are ordered from largest to smallest, so the first components capture the most significant patterns in the image. 

As more singular values and their corresponding columns of $U$ and $V$ are included, fewer components of the original image are omitted. Therefore, the reconstruction error decreases and the image becomes increasingly similar to the original Lenna image.
